PLFS September 2026: read the basis with the rate

The monthly bulletin puts unemployment at 5.0% for people aged 15 and above under Current Weekly Status. The denominator and survey window belong beside the figure.

Checked against the sources below on 17 October 2026. Items marked "background" are general knowledge, not taken from the sources.

Key facts

  • The National Statistical Office under MoSPI conducts the Periodic Labour Force Survey. The September bulletin reports all-India monthly indicators for people aged 15 and above using Current Weekly Status (CWS).
  • For September, the bulletin reports LFPR at 55.5%, WPR at 52.7% and unemployment at 5.0%. Each figure uses the CWS basis.
  • Urban LFPR rose from 50.4% in August to 50.7% in September; the all-India LFPR remained close to 55.5%. The bulletin also reports a 6.9% urban unemployment rate for September.
  • MoSPI revised the PLFS sampling design from January 2025 to support more frequent estimates. Its published comparison note says this can affect comparability with earlier estimates.

How to read the three indicators

LFPR counts people who are working or seeking or available for work as a share of the population. WPR counts employed people as a share of the population. The unemployment rate is the unemployed share of the labour force. The denominators differ, so 5.0% is not the share of the whole population without work.

CWS looks at the seven days before the survey. One month is a short window, so do not read a single change as a long-term trend.

Prelims lines

PLFS is conducted by the NSO, MoSPI. The September 2026 monthly bulletin uses CWS and covers people aged 15 and above. The sampling design changed from January 2025.

Mains angle

Use the bulletin to discuss how labour-market indicators are measured, then bring in participation, job quality and gender or rural–urban gaps. Compare like with like: keep the age group, status measure, reference period and post-2025 design in view.
